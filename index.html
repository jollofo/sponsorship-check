Does this role sponsor visas?

For each startup role, this shows what the posting itself says about sponsorship, with the sentence quoted. If a posting doesn't say it plainly, the answer is "Not stated", never a guess. Roles come live from the a16z speedrun jobs API.

      Rules, not AI: a role only gets a status other than "Not stated" when a sentence in its posting says so, and that sentence is shown. Equal-opportunity boilerplate ("regardless of citizenship") is ignored. Checks up to 300 roles at a time. Always confirm with the company. Built by James Muguiyi.
